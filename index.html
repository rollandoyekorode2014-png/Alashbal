Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

ALASHBAL International School (AIS) is committed to provide premium quality education and aims to develop the skills, talents, and intellectual intelligence of students as a solid foundation for future life.

Vision

ALASHBAL International School (AIS) envisions itself to be a prominent educational institution that is dedicated to nurture our brilliant YOUNG ACHIEVERS of today to be competitive global leaders of tomorrow.

From the Head of School

Welcome to Alashbal International School

It is with great pleasure and pride that I extend a warm welcome to you all to our vibrant and dynamic learning community at Alashbal International School (AIS). As the founding School Administrator, I am honored to lead an institution dedicated to excellence in education.

At AIS, we are committed to delivering a premium quality education that nurtures the intellectual intelligence, talents and skills of each student.

Roaring load,
